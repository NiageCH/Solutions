Traceability System — Refrigerant Distribution

End-to-end process · Hybrid architecture: RFID (warehouse) + NFC + QR (field)

⚙️  PHASE 1 — Warehouse: Cylinder receiving and sealing

Cylinder arrives at warehouse

Empty returned by a customer or recharged by an external supplier

→

RFID Portal — Entry

Reads all tags as cylinders pass through. Automatically registers IDs, status and origin in the cloud. No human intervention required.

RFID UHF Automatic
→

IoT Scale — Weigh-in

Each cylinder passes over the scale. Tag + weight are linked in the cloud instantly. No warehouse operator enters the weight manually.

RFID IoT Scale No operator
→

Dual NFC/UHF seal applied

Single-use breakaway collar placed on the cylinder valve. One tag that works with the RFID portal in the warehouse and with the driver's phone in the field.

NFC UHF Tamper-bit

📦  PHASE 3 — On route: Customer delivery
Customer generates the authorisation (before the visit)

Customer purchases in app

Selects gas type and quantity in kg. Pays online (card, transfer, etc.)

Customer app
→

Platform generates unique QR

Cryptographically signed token. Contains: gas type · authorised kg · order ID · expires in 24h · single use only.

Signed QR Single use
→

Customer receives QR

Push notification / SMS / WhatsApp. Customer shows it on screen to the driver at the point of delivery.

Customer phone
At the delivery point — two mandatory simultaneous reads

① Scans customer QR code

Using the camera on the driver's phone. Platform validates: valid token? already used? correct gas type? within allowed time window?

QR Cloud validation
+

② Taps phone on NFC seal

Phone reads the NFC tag on the valve. Platform confirms: is this the cylinder assigned to this customer? seal intact? tamper-bit = 0?

NFC Cloud validation
+

③ GPS Geofencing check

System verifies that the driver's GPS is within the radius of the customer's address. Prevents remote or fraudulent confirmations.

GPS Geofencing
Valid QR + intact NFC seal + GPS in zone + cylinder assigned to this customer — all 3 factors OK?

✓ Delivery authorised

Driver's app unlocks the "Confirm delivery" button. Customer may break the seal.

Technology legend

RFID UHF Fixed portal at warehouse — bulk contactless read at 1–3 m
NFC Driver's phone — individual seal read in the field
QR Customer app — signed, single-use order authorisation
IoT Scale Automated weighing connected to the cloud, no human input
Automatic Action with no human intervention, logged directly to the cloud
Alert Instant push notification to manager with full event detail
Cloud Data recorded in the central platform with timestamp and full traceability

The seal carries a single dual NFC+UHF tag: the warehouse RFID portal reads it at distance · the driver's phone reads it via NFC · one physical seal for the entire cylinder lifecycle.
